## EDUCATIONAL CONTENT ANGLES — CHICAGO

These are parking facts and local context worth publishing — no SpotShare pitch needed.
A property manager in Chicago would find this useful on its own.

**Chicago.gov / Birchwood Law / Thompson Coburn**
On September 25, 2025, Chicago eliminated minimum parking requirements for properties within 1/4 mile of a frequent bus route or 1/2 mile of a rail station. That covers approximately three-quarters of the city — including virtually every luxury condo corridor: River North, Gold Coast, Streeterville, South Loop, Lincoln Park. Underground parking in Chicago costs $67,500 per space to build. Without a legal minimum, most developers building in transit-adjacent neighborhoods will build fewer spaces, not more. Every new luxury condo that breaks ground under this ordinance is making a business decision about parking, not a code compliance one.
Content angle: What Chicago's September 2025 parking ordinance means for HOA boards in new luxury buildings — and why it's not going to be reversed.

**ParkChicago / CostCheckUSA / Windy Parking**
Parking near a Chicago condo is among the most expensive in any SpotShare market. Street meters in River North and the Loop peak at $7.75–$9.75/hour. Parking garages in downtown, River North, and Streeterville run $35–$52/day for daily visitors. Monthly parking ranges from $280/month (Loop fringe) to $475/month (Magnificent Mile). Effective January 1, 2025, the city's parking tax increased to 23.25% on daily lot parking — one of the highest rates in the country.
Content angle: The true cost of guest parking in Chicago in 2026 — why a once-a-week visitor is paying $150+ per month to park near your building.

**Illinois HOA Law Blog / Tressler LLP**
A change to Section 18.12 of the Illinois Condominium Property Act took effect January 1, 2025 and required all Illinois condo boards to adopt a formal accessible parking policy by April 2, 2025. The policy must include a procedure for requesting accessible parking and a decision timeline (maximum 45 days). This is notable not just for accessibility compliance, but because it placed parking allocation formally on every Illinois condo board's agenda in early 2025. Property managers who hadn't updated their parking policies are now overdue.
Content angle: What Illinois condo boards need to know about the new accessible parking requirement — and how it connects to the broader question of how parking is allocated in your building.

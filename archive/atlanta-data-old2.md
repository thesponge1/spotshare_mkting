## EDUCATIONAL CONTENT ANGLES — ATLANTA

These are parking facts and local context worth publishing — no SpotShare pitch needed.
A property manager in Atlanta would find this useful on its own.

**City of Atlanta / ATL Plus Mobility + Rough Draft Atlanta + Center for Civic Innovation**
Atlanta's city government is actively eliminating off-street parking minimums. The BeltLine Overlay District already eliminated minimums for new residential and commercial developments in February 2024. In 2025, Councilmember Jason Dozier introduced ATL Zoning 2.0, the city's first comprehensive zoning code overhaul since 1982, which would eliminate minimum parking requirements in virtually all residential zones citywide except single-family (R1–R3). The stated rationale: lower housing production costs and enable more affordable rents — not improve parking access.
Content angle: What Atlanta's parking minimum elimination actually means for HOA boards in new luxury buildings — fewer required spaces means guest parking becomes a board decision, not a code requirement.

**SpotAngels / Parkopedia / City of Atlanta Parking Guide**
Guest parking in Atlanta is not free and not easy to find. Street meters in downtown and Midtown run $2.00/hour with 2-hour enforcement limits Monday–Saturday, 7am–7pm. Monthly garage parking in Midtown averages $190/month (range: $75–$500). Daily parking runs $15–$30/day in premium downtown/Midtown locations. For a building that hosts regular guests, these costs add up quickly — and residents feel them.
Content angle: What guests actually pay to park near a Midtown or Buckhead condo in 2026, and why buildings that can offer a guest spot are at a real amenity advantage.

**Kolter Urban / SpaceInTown / Rough Draft Atlanta**
Elyse Buckhead is Atlanta's next major luxury condo project — 194 units in a 20-story tower at 102 West Paces Ferry Road, with site work starting Q2 2026 and delivery projected Q4 2028–Q1 2029. Units start in the mid-$900k range with 63,000 sq ft of amenity space. As a new build under ATL Zoning 2.0's changing framework, its parking allocation will be set by developer decision, not code requirement. This is exactly the kind of building where guest parking becomes a first board decision.
Content angle: Why the Buckhead luxury condo pipeline is worth watching for parking policy — and what new HOA boards at these towers will be deciding before their first resident meeting.

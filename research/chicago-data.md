**Illinois Condo Act Section 18.12 — April 2, 2025 Deadline Has Passed**
Every Illinois condominium association board was legally required to adopt a written accessible parking policy and procedure by April 2, 2025. Boards that missed this deadline face civil action exposure from unit owners and prospective owners. Multiple HOA law firms (Levin Ginsburg, Tressler LLP, LP Legal, Dickler Law) published alerts before the deadline. The law is now in effect — the education moment is compliance verification and what to do if a board hasn't acted.
Content angle: A plain-language guide for Chicago condo boards: "Illinois Condo Act Section 18.12: Is Your Building's Parking Policy Compliant? A Checklist for HOA Boards" — framed as a compliance resource, not an ad.

**Ordinance O2025-0015577 — Chicago's September 25, 2025 Parking Reform**
Chicago eliminated parking minimums for approximately 74% of the city effective September 25, 2025 — any development within ½ mile of CTA/Metra or ¼ mile of key bus routes can now be built with zero required parking. Legal alerts from Taft Law, Thompson Coburn, and Birchwood Law are the authoritative summaries. The financial driver: underground parking costs $67,500 per space, equivalent to the cost of adding an entire residential unit.
Content angle: A guide for Chicago condo HOA boards: "What Chicago's New Parking Ordinance Means for Your Building's Guest Parking Policy" — framed around what existing buildings face when neighbors build parking-free next door.

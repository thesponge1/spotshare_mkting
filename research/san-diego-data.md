**Source:** rethinkdowntown.com — Downtown San Diego Parking Costs 2025: Complete Rate Guide

Street meters doubled to $2.50/hour on January 31, 2025 and run 8am–10pm seven days a week. The city activated $10/hour event zone pricing across 200+ blocks (East Village, Gaslamp, Marina) for Padres games and events with 10,000+ attendance. Monthly garage parking now runs $200–$400/month in the core; daily rates $15–$35. Rates are projected to climb to $3.00–$3.50/hour by late 2026. Credit card transaction fee of $0.35 per payment added November 2025.

Content angle: "What does it actually cost your guests to park when they visit your downtown San Diego condo in 2026?" — a practical rate-by-scenario breakdown for property managers to share with residents who ask about visitor parking costs.

---

**Source:** City of San Diego / Inside San Diego — June 2025 Comprehensive Parking Reform Package; 2026 Land Development Code Update

On June 2, 2025, City Council approved a comprehensive package of parking reforms: dynamic pricing tied to demand, seven-day enforcement, extended hours to new neighborhoods (North Park, South Park, Normal Heights, College Area), and updated valet/construction permit fees. Separately, Ordinance O-21989 amendments to parking regulations took effect August 22, 2025 outside the Coastal Zone — with Coastal Zone approval expected in 2026. The 2026 Land Development Code Update (139 total amendments, 32 Downtown-specific) is in final review for spring 2026 adoption.

Content angle: "Why San Diego's 2025–2026 parking reforms make guest parking management a permanent amenity decision for downtown HOA boards" — explains the regulatory cascade and what it means for buildings that still haven't systematized guest parking.

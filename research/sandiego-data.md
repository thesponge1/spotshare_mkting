## EDUCATIONAL CONTENT ANGLES — San Diego

These are parking facts and local context worth publishing about — no SpotShare pitch needed. A property manager in San Diego would find this useful on its own.

### 1. Downtown San Diego parking costs just got more expensive — and it's not done

**Source:** rethinkdowntown.com — Downtown San Diego Parking Costs 2025: Complete Rate Guide

Street meter rates doubled to $2.50/hour in 2025. The city launched $10/hour event pricing for Padres games and concerts across ~200 blocks in East Village, Gaslamp, and Marina District. Rates are projected to climb to $3.00–3.50/hour by late 2026, and dynamic pricing is rolling out. Balboa Park daily rates hit $16 starting early 2026.

**Content angle:** "What does it actually cost your guests to park when they visit your downtown San Diego condo in 2026?" — a practical breakdown for property managers to share with residents who complain about visitor parking complaints.

### 2. San Diego's parking reform is quietly shrinking guest parking in new buildings

**Source:** sandiego.gov — Parking Reform program page

San Diego eliminated minimum parking requirements for commercial properties in Transit Priority Areas in 2022. A new amendment to the municipal parking code took effect March 11, 2026. The city's explicit goal is reducing parking supply to encourage transit use. New condo developments in TPAs can now be built with fewer total spaces — which directly reduces the guest parking ratio available in newer buildings.

**Content angle:** "Why newer San Diego condos are being built with less parking — and what HOA boards should do about it" — explains the regulatory trend and positions proactive guest parking management as a forward-looking amenity decision.

## EDUCATIONAL CONTENT ANGLES — Atlanta

These are parking facts and local context worth publishing about — no SpotShare pitch needed. A property manager in Atlanta would find this useful on its own.

### 1. Atlanta's street parking rules hit guests harder than most cities

**Source:** SpotAngels — 2026 Atlanta Street Parking Expert Guide

Atlanta has strict zone-based enforcement: 2-hour limits in business/government zones (Mon–Sat, 7 AM–7 PM), up to 4-hour limits in entertainment districts. In residential neighborhoods, guests must obtain separate guest passes to park legally overnight — residents with permits cannot simply host visitors on the street without a pass. Meter violations start at $35 and escalate to $95 at 45 days.

**Content angle:** "What your guests are actually dealing with when they try to park near your Atlanta condo" — a practical guide for HOA boards and property managers who get resident complaints about visitor parking, explaining why the street is not a reliable fallback.

### 2. Buckhead and Midtown have a luxury condo boom underway — and HOA boards are brand new

**Source:** Rough Draft Atlanta / Urbanize Atlanta — Buckhead condo pipeline 2024–2026

Kolter Urban has a 198-unit condo tower at 102 West Paces Ferry in Buckhead under development (448 parking spaces), plus Elyse Buckhead launching with units starting in the mid-$900,000s. Active development pipeline across Midtown and Buckhead means a wave of new luxury condo buildings with newly-formed HOA boards making their first amenity decisions in 2025–2026.

**Content angle:** "Atlanta's new luxury condos are being built right — but what happens in year two when HOA boards start hearing guest parking complaints?" — frames SpotShare as the natural first amenity add for a building that has everything else figured out.

### 3. Atlanta luxury condo HOA fees: what boards are actually paying for (and what's missing)

**Source:** Atlanta Skyrise Blog — "The Truth About Condo HOA Fees in Atlanta Luxury Buildings" (April 27, 2026)

A very fresh breakdown of what Atlanta luxury condo HOA fees cover: building insurance (now a major cost due to new Fannie Mae/Freddie Mac lending requirements), concierge, valet, fitness centers, pools, reserves. Parking management is conspicuously absent from the standard amenities list.

**Content angle:** "The one amenity missing from most Atlanta luxury condo HOA budgets" — targets HOA boards reviewing their fee structure who want to look like heroes to residents.
